KillingMist Bingo MixItUp Messages (Private Use)

SHORT MESSAGE
How to play Bingo: Redeem channel points for a Bingo card link, stamp only called options, then tap Claim Bingo on your card when your pattern is valid.

LONG MESSAGE
KillingMist Bingo quick guide: 1) Redeem the channel points Bingo card reward. 2) Open your personal card link. 3) Stamp only options that have been called. 4) Complete the active pattern (rows/corners/blackout/postage). 5) Tap Claim Bingo on your card page. A round starts with no called options, and claims are server-validated.

OPTIONAL ADD-ON
Need a new card link? Redeem the channel points reward again.
